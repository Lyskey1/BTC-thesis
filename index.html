The evidence

Fourteen years, one rhythm.

Every ~4 years the halving cuts new supply in half. The same sequence follows each time: a bull run, a top 17 to 18 months later, a fall that lasts about a year, then quiet accumulation.

The rhythm

About three years up, one year down.

Since 2015 every rally ran ~1,060 days from bottom to top (1,068, 1,061, 1,050), and every fall about a year (363, then 376). This fall is at 411 days. If the rhythm holds, the next 1,060-day rally starts in the bottom window and tops out in late 2029.

The asymmetry: buying three months early or late costs little, because prices flatline near the bottom. Selling three months late is expensive, because the fall after a top runs about a year. So buy over a wide window, and start selling before the top.

What could break the rhythm

Three risks this cycle.

Quantum computers

A large enough quantum computer could forge Bitcoin's signatures. Coins whose public keys are already exposed go first, and moving to post-quantum signatures takes a soft fork and years of coordination.

Credible alternatives

New stores of value compete for the same "digital gold" money: Zcash with private money, Pearl with proof-of-work mining tied to AI compute. Every dollar they win is demand Bitcoin doesn't get.

AI finding bugs

AI is getting very good at finding bugs. Bitcoin upgrades slowly on purpose, so its whole stack (nodes, wallets, libraries) may not patch as fast as new holes are found.

How the bottom window and zone are set

Time. The longest mature bear ran 411 days (Nov 29, 2013 → Jan 14, 2015). The window is that length ±5% for the core (day 390–432, Oct 31 → Dec 12, 2026) and ±10% for the outer edge (day 370–452, Oct 11, 2026 → Jan 1, 2027).

Depth. A target of $35K–$58K, which is −54% to −72% from the $126,198 top. Past bears went −94%, −87%, −84% and −77.5%; a shallower floor continues that trend.

The two lines. The top line is the best straight fit (linear price scale) through the 2017, 2021 and 2025 tops; each top sits within $2.8K of it, and it reads $178K–$180K in Sep–Oct 2029. The 2029 top zone runs from the ×1.8 halving multiple (~$165K) up to that line: $165K–$180K, Sep–Oct 2029. The floor line runs from the Nov 21, 2022 bottom ($15,476) to $35K today, the floor of the target zone, and rises about $4.9K a year.

Which one wins. Bitcoin bottoms on time or on depth, whichever comes first.

Is this financial advice?

No. Four cycles are not a statistic, and every cycle has broken the previous one's script somewhere. This page exists for the record, and for the fun of it. Do your own research.
